Jitro

Privacy Policy

Effective October 11, 2026

Jitro turns photos of round things into a looping video. It is built to keep your photos yours. In short: we don't collect any data about you, and your photos never reach us.

What we collect

Nothing. Jitro has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We don't run servers that receive your photos or any information about how you use the app.

Camera and photos

Jitro uses your camera to find round objects and take photos of them. Photos are processed entirely on your device. If you choose photos from your library, only the photos you pick are shared with Jitro, through Apple's photo picker. When you export a loop, Jitro saves it to your photo library; it asks for permission to add photos and cannot read the rest of your library.

iCloud

If iCloud sync is on (it can be turned off in Jitro's Settings), your hunts are stored in Jitro's private folder in your own iCloud account, so they appear on your other devices. This data is held by Apple under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. We can't see or access it.

Purchases

Jitro Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; we never see your payment details. The app checks with Apple whether you own Pro.

Sharing

When you share a loop to another app (for example TikTok or Instagram), that app's own privacy policy applies to what you share there.

Children

Jitro does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.

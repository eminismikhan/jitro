Common questions

How do I make a loop?
Tap New hunt, point the ring at something round and hold still (Auto) or tap the shutter (Manual). Collect as many as you like, then tap Make loop.

A circle is in the wrong place.
Open the hunt, tap the photo and drag or pinch the circle to fix it. You can also leave a photo out of the loop or delete it.

How do I restore Jitro Pro?
Settings in Jitro > Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel the yearly subscription?
iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions > Jitro. Cancel at least 24 hours before it renews.

My hunts don't show on my other device.
Turn on iCloud sync in Jitro's Settings on both devices, sign in to the same Apple Account, and make sure iCloud Drive is on.
